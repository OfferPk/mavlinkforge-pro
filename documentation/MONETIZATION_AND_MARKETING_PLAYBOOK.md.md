# MAVLinkForge Pro Monetization & Marketing Playbook

## 1\. Commercial Opportunity

The commercial drone, defense UAV, and eVTOL urban air mobility markets rely almost universally on MAVLink 2.0 for ground control communication. Avionics developers and payload manufacturers require lightweight protocol verification tools that eliminate the friction of compiling multi-gigabyte C++ ground control station codebases.

## 2\. Pricing Strategy & Revenue Model

- **Drone Systems Engineer License**: \$99/month (Interactive studio, standalone Python CLI, XML message definition importer).  
- **OEM & Flight Testing Lab License**: \$990/month (\$8,500/year perpetual) including custom dialect generator, radio hardware serial bridging, automated CI/CD packet fuzzers, and priority engineering support.
